An architectural breakdown of the deep learning architectures, spatial formulations, graph algorithms, and multi-criteria heuristics evaluated and designed for ResQRoute AI.

01 // CNN
Application: AI Accident Detection

ResNet-18 (Convolutional Neural Network)

A deep residual convolutional neural network used to classify incoming CCTV video frames into two discrete operational states: Background or Near Accident Event. Utilizes an 18-layer deep architecture featuring residual shortcut connections that bypass layers to mitigate the vanishing gradient problem.

03 // Probability
Application: Accident Event Classification

Softmax Classification

Converts raw unnormalized logit outputs from the final linear layer into a bounded, normalized probability distribution summing to 1. Enables threshold-based identification between benign background frames and suspected near-accident events.

04 // Graph Routing
Application: Ambulance & Hospital Road Navigation

Dijkstra's Algorithm / A*

Foundational shortest-path and heuristic search algorithms utilized in road network graph traversals. Evaluates road segment weights, traffic costs, and distance heuristics to compute optimal travel trajectories between ambulances, incident sites, and destination emergency rooms.

Graph Cost Optimization f(n) = g(n) + h(n)
Nodes: [Ambulance] → [Intersections A, B, C] → [Hospital]
Cost: min Σ(Segment_Length / Speed_Limit + Congestion_Penalty)
*Actual implementation engine depends on deployed routing service (e.g. OSRM / Mapbox / GraphHopper).
05 // Geodesics
Application: Geographical Distance Calculation

Haversine Formula

Determines the great-circle distance between two latitude and longitude coordinate pairs across a spherical surface. Provides computationally instantaneous straight-line proximity estimates between moving emergency vehicles and reported crash coordinates.

Spherical Great-Circle Formulation Radius R = 6,371 km
a = sin²(Δφ/2) + cos(φ₁)·cos(φ₂)·sin²(Δλ/2)
d = 2 · R · atan2(√a, √(1−a))
Ideal for fast O(1) spatial pre-filtering prior to road graph routing.
06 // Optimization
Application: Ambulance Allocation

Greedy Selection

An algorithmic decision strategy that evaluates the pool of currently available, active emergency units and assigns the candidate with the minimum estimated arrival time (ETA) or shortest spatial distance to the incident scene without waiting for future global state shifts.

07 // Decision Science
Application: Hospital Recommendation

Multi-Criteria Decision-Making (MCDM)

A holistic evaluation framework that ranks potential receiving medical facilities across multiple competing criteria: real-time general bed availability, critical ICU bed capacity, specialized emergency capability levels, live road distance, and estimated transit duration.

Weighted Parameter Assessment Vector Score
Score = w₁(ICU_Avail) + w₂(Bed_Capacity) + w₃(Specialty) - w₄(Travel_Time)
Prevents routing severe emergency cases to full or unprepared emergency clinics.

End-to-End Orchestration

Complete System Workflow

From the initial visual frame input on traffic cameras to final patient handover at an equipped emergency medical center: a 10-stage coordinated pipeline combining AI detection and human verification.

01

Accident Video Frame Input

Phase 1: Ingestion

Continuous live CCTV surveillance streams and road sensor video feeds are ingested into the processing pipeline and split into discrete, normalized image frames for real-time analysis.

CCTV Ingestion Stream / Frame Buffering
02

ResNet-18 Accident Detection

Phase 1: Ingestion

Each extracted frame passes through the fine-tuned 18-layer residual neural network, leveraging trained spatial features to detect structural collision patterns and atypical road behavior.

Deep Learning / ResNet-18 Architecture
03

Confidence-Based Suspected Accident Identification

Phase 2: Validation

Softmax classification scores evaluate whether class probabilities for "Near Accident Event" breach the designated statistical confidence threshold, preventing false alarms from traffic anomalies.

Softmax Probability Thresholding / Event Flagging
04

Evidence Capture

Phase 2: Validation

The time-interval extraction protocol triggers automatically, archiving six sequential screenshot frames sampled at three-second intervals along with GPS coordinates and a unique Incident ID.

6 Frames @ 3-Second Delta / Incident ID Serialization
05

Ambulance Driver Verification

Phase 3: Human-in-the-Loop

The captured evidence screenshots appear on the assigned ambulance driver's terminal. The driver or dispatcher reviews the image sequence to confirm whether the alert is a valid physical crash.

Human-in-the-Loop Verification / False Positive Safeguard
06

Ambulance Allocation

Phase 4: Operations

Once verified, the dispatch engine evaluates active ambulances using greedy nearest-distance selection or minimal travel-time algorithms to assign the closest suitable emergency vehicle.

Greedy Selection / Fleet Availability State
07

Road-Network Navigation

Phase 4: Operations

Turn-by-turn routing is generated via road-network pathfinding engines (e.g. Dijkstra or A* variants), calculating optimal paths that account for current road geometry and traffic barriers.

Shortest-Path Graph Routing / Turn-by-Turn Telemetry
08

Police Coordination

Phase 4: Operations

Incident geolocation, visual evidence, and anticipated arrival vectors are automatically transmitted to local law enforcement dispatchers to assist with traffic clearing and road containment.

Inter-Agency Notification / Corridor Clearing
09

Hospital Recommendation

Phase 5: Medical Handover

Multi-criteria decision models evaluate candidate medical centers based on real-time bed counts, ICU readiness, distance, and transit time, recommending the most prepared facility for patient triage.

Multi-Criteria Decision-Making (MCDM) / Facility Match
10

Emergency Arrival and Handover

Phase 5: Medical Handover

The ambulance arrives on site, stabilizes the patient, and completes transfer to the pre-notified emergency department, closing the incident loop with full timestamped audit trails.

Academic transparency and technical accuracy are fundamental to ResQRoute AI. We explicitly distinguish between models that have been formally trained in our codebase versus architectural concepts that serve as conceptual or modular building blocks.

Confirmed Trained Model

Verified in project pipeline & codebase

  • ResNet-18 Deep Convolutional Network

  • Transfer Learning Methodology

Architectural design patterns & potential approaches

  • Dijkstra & A* Routing Paradigms

  • Haversine Distance Formulation

    Conceptual baseline for great-circle spherical distance computation. Utilized to illustrate spatial radius filtering without claiming proprietary geodesics.

  • Greedy Selection Heuristics

    Characterizes the nearest-available ambulance assignment logic as a heuristic concept, rather than an intractable constraint satisfaction solver.

  • Multi-Criteria Decision-Making (MCDM)

    Explains the multi-variable triage reasoning (beds, ICU, travel duration) without fabricating proprietary mathematical weighting equations unless confirmed by the source code.

Project Mission

Bridging Vision AI and Emergency Services

In medical emergencies, the golden hour dictates survivability. ResQRoute AI is engineered around a single conviction: Every Second Counts. Give Every Second a Purpose.

By continuously monitoring traffic camera feeds using efficient computer vision architectures, the system bridges the critical delay between an accident occurring and the first 911 dispatch call.

Crucially, ResQRoute AI implements a human-in-the-loop verification protocol. Before sirens blare and units deploy, assigned responders review immediate six-frame visual evidence, ensuring public safety resources are allocated with precision, reliability, and human oversight.

Real-Time Ingestion

Coordinated Dispatch

Seamless telemetry links between ambulance drivers, police control rooms, and hospital triage.

Triage-Aware Hospitals

Multi-variable evaluation matches patients to facilities with available ICU beds and specialist care.
